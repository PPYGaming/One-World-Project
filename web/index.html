HELP & BETA

Your forest survival guide.

Move: joystick / WASD. Auto-run: hold the joystick and drag up to show its lock target. Reach the target and release there to lock. Release anywhere else to stop. Touch the joystick again to stop auto-run. Hold and drag the eye to look without steering; release to return. Move joystick to stop. Shift on keyboard. Look: drag. Attack: right button / Space. Bag: B. Quick tools: 1-3.
Drink at the river. Approach a coloured gate, then open Map to travel. Sleep at your placed bedroll.
Beta: original lowpoly enemies and animals; visual quality and phone performance are still being tested.
