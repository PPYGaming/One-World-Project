ONE WORLD BETA 1.3.0 MODIFICATIONS
Original procedural penguin/camel, adult mutant silhouettes, undergrowth/berry/fibre geometry, transit gate geometry, interface vectors and effects were made for this private modified build. No ARK or The Forest artwork, code, map or story was used. Those games were visual-direction references only. Legacy Quaternius character files remain bundled for compatibility; the playable characters and face icon use the credited CC0 VRoid HairSample models.

Low-quality painted foliage textures:
assets/tex/normaltree-painted.png and pine-painted.png are modified from the bundled licensed tree leaf textures. Original tree geometry is unchanged. Small alpha-mask gaps are closed and colors muted for the Low painted variant. The original model/texture attribution and license above still apply.
