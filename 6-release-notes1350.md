# One World Beta 1.3.50

Testing release, versionCode 72. Install over the existing beta. Do not uninstall or clear app data.

- Connected snow ranges, broad terraced desert highland, central wooded stone ridges, and scattered swamp crags are integrated into the cumulative Second Map. Existing harvestable tree models are retained; lightweight distant canopy fills the wider landscape. All hidden slopes and metric dimensions are interpretations of the supplied illustration, not an exact reconstruction.
- Settings > Help & Beta > Beta inspection flight. Joystick/WASD travels, touch UP/DOWN or keyboard E/Q changes height, RETURN restores your original ground location. Flight does not overwrite that saved location, spend survival stats, or permit attacks.
- Existing local saves and profiles are archived once before this terrain update. No world, inventory, crafting station, resources or profile progress is intentionally reset. Saving is paused if the protective archive cannot be stored.
- New profiles default to Boy and require a name. Two-profile creation cap. Existing larger registries remain accessible. The other survivor's name replaces the plus button for direct switching, without a popup.

Local checks: actual packaged entry/readiness/menu/settings/campfire, flight movement and original-location save/return, profile switching/creation cap/save retention, source/APK payload checks, original signing certificate.

Not verified: physical Android FPS/thermal/long-session behavior, the earlier app exit cause, and full on-device collision traversal across the world. This beta is for the owner's installation review; it does not claim the earlier lag/crash is fixed or the illustration's detailed model quality is matched.

Original game: Smiley, modified and redistributed with permission. Bundled third-party notices retained. AI services supplied procedural draft ideas, not finished high-quality model assets. Claude could not see its attachment; its draft was description-based and the shipped terrain was independently revised. No paid service or generation credits used.
